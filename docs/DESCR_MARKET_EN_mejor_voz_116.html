HyperClose, plain

If you try to open a new trade while protection is active, HyperClose closes that exposure at once and records the attempt — including on OFF days. That cuts overtrading when you are already protected.

Open trade crossing midnight or the weekend

An open trade crossing midnight or the weekend stays under your agreed % — protection does not “reset” mid-trade. That is why the panel shows the intraday and calendar-crossing scenarios differently — and you see them as they are.

If protection was active and an open trade crosses midnight, the weekend, or another calendar boundary, TEVSYS does not set you “free” just because the day changed. You keep the protection with the percentages you locked while the trade continues and the program watches. On the panel: trade in progress / you stay protected when that applies. That is different from scheduling Monday with no open trade (SAVE / SCHEDULED).

When a limit is reached

  • Automatic close of open positions, with documented precision.
  • On profit, a small stability margin (~0.01% above your %) avoids closes on a single tick — by design.

FAQ

Does it close my positions if I hit my daily loss limit?
Yes. When the account reaches the agreed percentage, TEVSYS closes open positions, blocks new exposure, and leaves a record of the close.

How do I know how much risk remains before the close?
Click the panel: you see the agreed limit, progress (how much you have used), and the margin left until STOP. Intraday and overnight read according to the scenario — it is not one generic panel.

What if I leave a trade open past midnight?
If protection is active, you stay under the % you locked: protection does not reset mid-trade. On screen: trade in progress — you stay protected.

Is the demo complete?
Yes: full Advanced edition on a real terminal (not Strategy Tester only). It expires by time, not by features. You can configure, force a close, and cross-check records before you rent.

Does TEVSYS give signals or trade for me?
No. It only applies the limits you configure. The strategy is yours; tevsys enforces the pact and leaves evidence you can cross-check with MT5.
