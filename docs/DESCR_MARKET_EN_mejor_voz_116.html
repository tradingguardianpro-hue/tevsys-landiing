FAQ

Does it close my positions if I hit my daily loss limit?
Yes. When the account reaches the agreed percentage, TEVSYS closes open positions, blocks new exposure, and leaves a record of the close.

How do I know how much risk remains before the close?
Intraday: the panel shows the state of the day’s pact — limits and protection in view. Overnight / weekend: the panel shows that the trade stays under the pact and the calendar scenario (trade in progress / you stay protected). On click: you open the detail — agreed limit, how much you have used, and how much remains until STOP.

What if I leave a trade open past midnight?
If protection is active, you stay under the % you locked: protection does not reset mid-trade. On screen: trade in progress — you stay protected. You will see it in this listing’s screenshots.

What if I turn off the PC or VPS?
If the terminal drops, tick-by-tick watch pauses. What you locked and protection state persist and re-engage when you return. For continuous watch, load TEVSYS on a stable VPS — ideally near the broker.

Is the demo complete?
Yes: full Advanced on a real terminal (not Strategy Tester only). Expires by time, not by features.

Does TEVSYS give signals or trade for me?
No. It only applies the limits you configure. The strategy is yours; tevsys enforces the pact and leaves evidence you can cross-check with MT5.
